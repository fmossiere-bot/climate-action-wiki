Reinventing chocolate through fermentation

At the second edition of the Sustainable Pastry competition, organised by the Fondation pour la Cuisine Durable in Lyon, chef Jean-Guillaume Rupin took third place with an unusual creation: a chocolate-inspired dessert made without cocoa.

For this challenge, he used ingredients from Milatea, developed by Green Spot Technologies, one of the companies in the Team for the Planet portfolio. His dish showed that the richness and depth of chocolate can be recreated with new approaches to food. The dessert combined shiitake squash with miso, wild seaweed from Brittany, fermented strawberries and a reinvented cocoa base made with Milatea ingredients.

Fermentation is at the heart of the dish. It is a real lever for innovation: it develops complex flavours and opens the way to more sustainable food.

Congratulations to chef Jean-Guillaume Rupin for a result that shows how culinary creativity and innovation can come together to shape the food of tomorrow.

It is encouraging to see companies in the Team for the Planet portfolio contributing to this kind of practical, tasty progress towards more sustainable food.
