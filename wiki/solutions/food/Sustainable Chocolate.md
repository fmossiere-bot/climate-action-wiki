Reinventing chocolate through fermentation

At the second edition of the Sustainable Pastry competition, organised by the Fondation pour la Cuisine Durable in Lyon, chef Jean-Guillaume Rupin took third place with a surprising and innovative creation: a chocolate-inspired dessert made without cocoa.

To take on this challenge, he used ingredients from Milatea, developed by Green Spot Technologies, one of the companies in the Team for the Planet portfolio. His dish showed that it is possible to recreate the richness and depth of chocolate through new food approaches. The dessert combined shiitake squash with miso, wild seaweed from Brittany, fermented strawberries, and a reinvented cocoa base made with Milatea ingredients.

At the heart of this creation is fermentation, a real lever for innovation. It allows complex flavours to be developed while opening the way to more sustainable food.

Congratulations to chef Jean-Guillaume Rupin for a result that shows how culinary creativity and innovation can come together to shape the food of tomorrow.

It is encouraging to see Team for the Planet's companies in the portfolio which contribute to this kind of practical, tasty progress towards more sustainable food.
