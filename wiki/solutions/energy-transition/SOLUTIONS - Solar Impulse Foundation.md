# Solar Impulse Foundation: A Guide to Clean and Profitable Solutions

## What is it

The **Solar Impulse Foundation** is probably best known through its leader, Bertrand Piccard, who became famous for flying around the world in a solar-powered plane. At heart, though, the foundation is a platform. It lists more than 1,000 clean solutions that already exist and make commercial sense, now 1,747 of them, and uses that list to make the case directly to governments and agencies.

Piccard and the foundation set out to show that environmental problems can be tackled without holding back economic growth. They identified over 1,000 clean and profitable solutions and have kept adding to the list since. The foundation offers political and economic decision-makers a practical "Guide to Solutions" that can be rolled out at scale. The aim is to help them draw up a roadmap towards more ambitious energy and environmental programmes, and in the end reach their carbon neutrality targets.

## Why it matters

A common barrier to climate action is the belief that solving environmental problems means giving up economic growth. The Solar Impulse Foundation focuses on solutions that are both clean and profitable, and makes them easy for policymakers and businesses to search. That speaks directly to the objection, and shows that credible options that can scale already exist across many sectors.

## Key facts

- The foundation was co-founded by Bertrand Piccard, known for flying a solar-powered aircraft around the world.
- It has identified more than 1,000 clean and profitable solutions to environmental problems, now totalling 1,747.
- Its Solutions Explorer tool lets political and economic decision-makers search this list directly.
- The foundation's stated aim is to help decision-makers build a roadmap towards more ambitious energy and environmental policy, in support of carbon neutrality goals.
