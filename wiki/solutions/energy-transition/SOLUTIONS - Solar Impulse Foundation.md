# Solar Impulse Foundation: A Guide to Clean and Profitable Solutions

## What is it

The **Solar Impulse Foundation** is probably best known through its leader, Bertrand Piccard, who became famous for flying around the world on a solar-powered plane. But at its core, the foundation is a platform that catalogues more than 1,000 existing, commercially viable clean solutions, now numbering 1,747, and uses that catalogue to advocate directly to governments and agencies.

To address environmental challenges without compromising economic growth, Piccard and the Solar Impulse Foundation identified over 1,000 clean and profitable solutions, and have continued expanding that list since. By offering political and economic decision-makers a practical "Guide to Solutions" that can be implemented at scale, the foundation aims to help them build a roadmap towards more ambitious energy and environmental programmes, and ultimately reach their carbon neutrality objectives.

## Why it matters

One of the persistent barriers to climate action is the perception that solving environmental problems requires sacrificing economic growth. By focusing specifically on solutions that are both clean and profitable, and making that list easy for policymakers and businesses to search, the Solar Impulse Foundation targets exactly that objection, offering evidence that credible, scalable options already exist across many sectors.

## Key facts

- The foundation was co-founded by Bertrand Piccard, known for piloting a solar-powered aircraft around the world.
- It has identified more than 1,000 clean and profitable solutions to environmental challenges, now totalling 1,747.
- Its Solutions Explorer tool lets political and economic decision-makers search this catalogue directly.
- The foundation's stated aim is to help decision-makers build a roadmap towards more ambitious energy and environmental policy, in support of carbon neutrality goals.
