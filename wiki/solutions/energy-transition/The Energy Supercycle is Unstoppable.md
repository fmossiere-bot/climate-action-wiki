# The Energy Supercycle Is Unstoppable

## What is it

A big change is under way in how the world produces and uses energy. It is often called an **energy "supercycle"**: a long-term shift towards electricity and clean power generation. Electricity is becoming the base of nearly every modern system, from transport to industry to data centres. Artificial intelligence plays a central part. It is a challenge, because it pushes electricity demand up quickly, and also a help, because it opens up new ways to save energy and make the grid more resilient.

## Why it matters

Calling the energy transition a "supercycle" rather than a single technology shift captures something useful. Transport, industry, buildings and computing are all moving onto electricity as their shared source of energy, at the same time. Seeing electrification as the thread that links these sectors explains why demand for clean power, and for grid capacity to deliver it reliably, is rising so fast in so many parts of the economy at once.

## Key facts

- The shift towards electricity and clean power generation is described as a long-term, structural "supercycle" rather than a short-term trend.
- Electricity is becoming the shared source of energy across transport, industry and data centres.
- Artificial intelligence cuts both ways: it drives a steep rise in electricity demand from data centres, while also making new efficiency gains and a more resilient grid possible.
