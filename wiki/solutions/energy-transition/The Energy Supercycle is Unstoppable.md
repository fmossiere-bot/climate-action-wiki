# The Energy Supercycle Is Unstoppable

## What is it

A revolutionary change is underway in how the world produces and uses energy: an **energy "supercycle"**, a profound, long-term shift towards electrification and clean energy generation. Electricity is becoming the foundation of nearly all modern systems, from transport to industry to data centres. Central to this transformation is the rise of artificial intelligence, which presents both a challenge, in the form of rapidly increasing electricity demand, and a solution, as it unlocks new possibilities for energy efficiency and grid resilience.

## Why it matters

Framing the energy transition as a "supercycle" rather than a single technology shift captures something important: this is not one sector switching fuels, it is transport, industry, buildings and computing all converging on electricity as their common energy carrier at the same time. Understanding electrification as the connective thread across these sectors helps explain why demand for clean power, and for the grid capacity to deliver it reliably, is rising so fast across so many parts of the economy simultaneously.

## Key facts

- The shift towards electrification and clean energy generation is described as a long-term structural "supercycle" rather than a short-term trend.
- Electricity is becoming the common energy carrier across transport, industry and data centres.
- Artificial intelligence is a double-edged factor in this shift: it drives a sharp rise in electricity demand from data centres, while also enabling new efficiency gains and improved grid resilience.
