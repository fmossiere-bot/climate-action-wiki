# About Carbon Accounting and GHG Scopes

When companies count their greenhouse gas emissions, they sort them into three groups called **Scopes**, following the internationally recognised GHG Protocol. Scope 1 is direct emissions from things the company owns or controls, such as fuel burned on site or in company vehicles. Scope 2 is indirect emissions from the electricity, heat or steam it buys. Scope 3 is every other indirect emission in its value chain, from the goods it buys, to staff travel, to the use of the products it sells.

## Fuel and Scope 1

One point from UCD course material on carbon accounting is easy to get wrong: **fuel burned on site, including natural gas, counts as Scope 1, not Scope 2**. Scope 2 is only for indirect emissions from purchased electricity. Natural gas used on the premises, for heating or industrial processes, is a direct emission because the company is doing the burning itself, even though it bought the gas from a supplier. [Compare Your Footprint's explainer on the difference between Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions](https://www.compareyourfootprint.com/difference-scope-1-2-3-emissions/) confirms this.
