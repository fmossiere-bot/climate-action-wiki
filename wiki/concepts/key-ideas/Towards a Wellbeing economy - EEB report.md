# Towards a Wellbeing Economy: EEB Report

## What it is
*"Towards a wellbeing economy that serves people and nature"* is a report by the **European Environmental Bureau (EEB)** and Oxfam Germany, written as part of the [Climate of Change project](https://climateofchange.info/). It argues that the European Union's dependence on economic growth is failing even by its own standards. According to the report, this model brings prosperity only to a small group at the top of the economic pyramid, while damaging the environment and driving global warming.

## Why it matters
Most EU policymaking assumes that GDP can keep growing while staying within environmental limits. The report questions that assumption. For anyone trying to follow the debate about degrowth and post-growth economics, it offers a concrete policy plan as well as a critique.

## The three pillars of a wellbeing economy
The report sets out a plan for moving to a wellbeing economy, built on three main pillars, which it calls **the three Ds**: dismantling exploitative economic structures, democratising how the economy is governed, and degrowing the economy.
