# Towards a Wellbeing Economy — EEB Report

## What it is
*"Towards a wellbeing economy that serves people and nature"* is a report by the **European Environmental Bureau (EEB)** and **Oxfam Germany**, produced in the context of the [Climate of Change project](https://climateofchange.info/). **Its central argument is that the European Union's addiction to economic growth is failing even on its own terms**: the report finds this model delivers prosperity only to a small group at the top of the economic pyramid, while trashing the environment and fuelling global warming.

## Why it matters
The report challenges the assumption, embedded in most EU policymaking, that continued GDP growth is compatible with staying within environmental limits. For anyone trying to understand the debate around degrowth and post-growth economics, it offers a concrete policy blueprint rather than just a critique.

## The three pillars of a wellbeing economy
The report sets out a blueprint for transitioning to a wellbeing economy, built on three main pillars, referred to as **the three Ds**: **dismantling** exploitative economic structures, **democratising** economic governance, and **degrowing** the economy.
