# Companies and Communities Are Driving Sustainability, Not Governments

In a winter 2024 interview with the French magazine *Ecologie360*, climate scientist **François Gemenne** argued that the push towards the world's 2050 climate targets is coming more and more from businesses and communities, and less from national governments. Gemenne is a professor at Sciences Po and HEC in Paris, a researcher at the University of Liège, and one of the lead authors of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change's Sixth Assessment Report (AR6).

## Why it matters

Government climate policy tends to move slowly, shaped by election cycles and competing short-term priorities. Gemenne points to a shift that is already happening. Companies are responding to regulation, investors and customers, and communities are organising locally. In many cases they are moving faster than the governments that set the official targets. Climate progress does not depend on any one level of government, and action led by companies and communities is worth following in its own right.
