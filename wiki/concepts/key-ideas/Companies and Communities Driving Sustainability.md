# Companies and Communities Are Driving Sustainability, Not Governments

In a winter 2024 interview with the French magazine *Ecologie360*, climate scientist **François Gemenne** argued that momentum towards the world's 2050 climate targets is increasingly coming from businesses and communities rather than from national governments. Gemenne is a professor at Sciences Po and HEC in Paris, a researcher at the University of Liège, and one of the lead authors of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change's Sixth Assessment Report (AR6).

## Why it matters

Government climate policy tends to move slowly, shaped by electoral cycles and competing short-term priorities. Gemenne's interview points to a shift already under way: companies responding to regulation, investors and customer pressure, and communities organising at a local level, are in many cases moving faster than the states that set the formal targets. It is a useful reminder that climate progress does not rest on any single level of government, and that corporate and community-led action are worth tracking in their own right.
