# Myth: Buying local matters more than cutting down on meat

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

What you eat matters far more than how far it travelled. For beef, transport is under 1% of the footprint.

## Why it sounds right

Buying local is genuinely good for other reasons: local economies, freshness, seasonal eating, and knowing how your food was produced. It is just not where the carbon is.

## What is actually true

Transport is a small share of food emissions overall and almost negligible for the highest impact foods. For beef, transport accounts for well under 1% of the footprint, with nearly all of it coming from land use and methane from the animals themselves. WRI's analysis finds beef uses around 20 times the land and produces around 20 times the emissions of beans per gram of protein. The 2025 EAT-Lancet report found a planetary health diet would cut non CO2 agricultural emissions by 15 to 34%, free up an area of land the size of India, and prevent around 15 million deaths a year. LSHTM and Cornell modelling published in Nature found a healthier global diet could cut land use emissions 85% by 2050.

## If they push back

**"Irish grass fed beef is different"**

It is better than feedlot beef on some measures, and still many times the footprint of plants. Less and better is the honest version.

## Also heard as

- "food miles are the problem"
- "local beef is fine"
- "it's the transport that does the damage"
