# Myth: EVs just move the pollution to the power station

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

Even on Poland's coal heavy grid an EV cuts emissions by about 40%, because the motor is four times more efficient. And it gets cleaner every year. A petrol car never does.

## Why it sounds right

The electricity does have to come from somewhere, and on a coal heavy grid an EV genuinely is dirtier than it would be on a clean one. The question is whether it is still better than burning petrol, and it is.

## What is actually true

An electric motor is roughly four times more efficient than a combustion engine, so an EV wins even where the grid is dirty. Lifecycle emissions fall by about two fifths in coal heavy Poland and two thirds in the UK. In China, where coal supplied 61% of power in 2022, an EV still breaks even after about 22,000 miles. The 2021 ICCT global study found cuts of 60 to 70% in the US and Europe, 35 to 45% in China and 20 to 35% in India. And an EV keeps improving as the grid decarbonises, while a petrol car is as clean on day one as it will ever be.

## If they push back

**"Not where I live, our grid is fossil heavy"**

Fair, the saving is smaller there. It is still a saving, and it grows every year without you doing anything.

## Also heard as

- "the electricity has to come from somewhere"
- "they run on coal"
- "long tailpipe"
