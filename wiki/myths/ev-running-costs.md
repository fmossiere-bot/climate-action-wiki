# Myth: EVs will soon cost more to run than petrol cars

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

If you charge at home overnight it is not close. Public rapid charging is where the gap narrows, and most people rarely use it.

## Why it sounds right

There is a real case buried here. If you rely entirely on public rapid charging, the cost per mile can approach petrol, and that is where these stories come from.

## What is actually true

Carbon Brief's comparison found EV drivers save on fuel in every country analysed, from the UK and Norway to the US, China, India, Argentina and Australia. Home charging on an off peak tariff costs substantially less than public fast charging, and most drivers charge at home overnight. The UK Climate Change Committee concluded EVs will be significantly cheaper than petrol and diesel vehicles to own and operate over their lifetimes, once servicing and fuel are both counted.

## If they push back

**"They will tax it eventually"**

Probably, since fuel duty has to come from somewhere. That is a policy design question, and it does not change the energy cost underneath.

## Also heard as

- "electricity prices will catch up"
- "public charging is more expensive than petrol"
- "wait until they tax the electricity"
