# Myth: EVs will soon cost more to run than petrol cars

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

If you charge at home overnight, it isn't close: the EV is much cheaper. Public rapid charging is where the gap narrows, and most people rarely use it.

## Why it sounds right

There is a real point buried here. If you rely entirely on public rapid chargers, the cost per mile can get close to petrol, and that is where these stories come from.

## What is actually true

Carbon Brief's comparison found EV drivers save on fuel in every country it looked at, from the UK and Norway to the US, China, India, Argentina and Australia. Charging at home on an off-peak tariff costs much less than public fast charging, and most drivers charge at home overnight. The UK Climate Change Committee concluded that EVs will be significantly cheaper than petrol and diesel cars to own and run over their lifetimes, once both servicing and fuel are counted.

## If they push back

**"They will tax it eventually"**

Probably, since the money from fuel duty has to be replaced somehow. That's a question of tax policy, and it doesn't change the underlying energy cost, which stays lower for EVs.

## Also heard as

- "electricity prices will catch up"
- "public charging is more expensive than petrol"
- "wait until they tax the electricity"
