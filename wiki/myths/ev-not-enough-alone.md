# Myth: Electric cars alone won't solve climate change

> [!WARNING] Verdict: True but incomplete

## Say this

I completely agree, they're about half the answer for transport. The other half is fewer and smaller cars and better public transport. It was never one or the other.

## Why it sounds right

It's true as stated, and it's a fair warning against imagining we can swap every car one for one and carry on exactly as before. Someone saying this is often closer to your view than they sound.

## What is actually true

It only becomes a problem when it's used as a reason to do nothing. The IPCC judges EVs likely to be essential for cutting transport emissions. It finds that new technology could deliver about **50%** of land transport emissions cuts by 2050, within a range of 30 to 70%. Changes to infrastructure contribute around 30% and changes in behaviour around 5%. So EVs are roughly half the answer, and fewer cars, smaller cars and better public transport make up the rest. We need both.

## If they push back

**"So why push EVs at all?"**

Because half is a lot, and because most people are not giving up their car this decade. We can do the easier half while we work on the harder half.

## Also heard as

- "swapping every car for an electric one isn't the answer"
- "it's just consumerism with a green badge"
- "we need to drive less, not drive electric"
