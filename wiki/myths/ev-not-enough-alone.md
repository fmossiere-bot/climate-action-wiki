# Myth: Electric cars alone won't solve climate change

> [!WARNING] Verdict: True but incomplete

## Say this

Completely agree, they are about half the answer for transport. The other half is fewer and smaller cars and better public transport. It was never one or the other.

## Why it sounds right

This is true as stated, and it is a fair warning against imagining we can swap every car one for one and carry on exactly as before. Someone saying this is often closer to you than they sound.

## What is actually true

It becomes a problem only when it is used as a reason to do nothing. The IPCC judges EVs likely crucial for decarbonising transport, and finds technology adoption could deliver about 50% of land transport emissions reductions by 2050, with a range of 30 to 70%. Infrastructure change contributes around 30% and behaviour change around 5%. So EVs are roughly half the answer, and fewer cars, smaller cars and better public transport are the rest. Both, not either.

## If they push back

**"So why push EVs at all?"**

Because half is a lot, and because most people are not giving up their car this decade. We can do the easy half while we fight for the hard half.

## Also heard as

- "swapping every car for an electric one isn't the answer"
- "it's just consumerism with a green badge"
- "we need to drive less, not drive electric"
