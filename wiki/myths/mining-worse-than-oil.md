# Myth: All that mining is worse for the planet than fossil fuels

> [!WARNING] Verdict: Mostly false

## Say this

Mining does real harm and that should be fixed. The difference is you mine for a turbine once, and you mine for a coal plant every single day it runs.

## Why it sounds right

Mining causes real environmental and social harm, including water use, habitat loss and serious human rights abuses in some supply chains. Anyone raising this deserves to be told they are right about that part.

## What is actually true

The comparison still does not hold. Even counting waste rock, the energy transition involves less material extraction than today's fossil fuel economy does. The decisive difference is that it is a one time build: a wind turbine's materials are dug up once, installed, and can later be recycled, whereas coal, oil and gas must be extracted and burned continuously, forever. Fossil fuel extraction is also not exactly clean or free of human rights problems, it is just older and less discussed.

## If they push back

**"So you accept the harm as long as it's green"**

No. Push for traceable supply chains and recycling. That fight is worth having and it is not an argument for burning more gas meanwhile.

## Also heard as

- "child labour in cobalt mines"
- "lithium mining destroys the environment"
- "it's just swapping one extraction for another"
