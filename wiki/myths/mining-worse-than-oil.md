# Myth: All that mining is worse for the planet than fossil fuels

> [!WARNING] Verdict: Mostly false

## Say this

Mining does real harm and that should be fixed. The difference is you mine for a turbine once, and you mine for a coal plant every single day it runs.

## Why it sounds right

Mining causes real environmental and social harm: it uses water, destroys habitat, and some supply chains involve serious human rights abuses. Anyone raising this is right about that part, and it's fair to say so.

## What is actually true

The comparison still doesn't hold. Even counting waste rock, the energy transition involves less material extraction than today's fossil fuel economy. The biggest difference is that it's a **one-time build**. A wind turbine's materials are dug up once, installed, and can later be recycled. Coal, oil and gas have to be extracted and burned continuously, forever. Fossil fuel extraction isn't clean or free of human rights problems either. It's simply older and talked about less.

## If they push back

**"So you accept the harm as long as it's green"**

No. Push for traceable supply chains and recycling. That fight is worth having, and it isn't a reason to burn more gas in the meantime.

## Also heard as

- "child labour in cobalt mines"
- "lithium mining destroys the environment"
- "it's just swapping one extraction for another"
