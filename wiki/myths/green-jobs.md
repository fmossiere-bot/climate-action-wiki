# Myth: The transition will destroy jobs

> [!WARNING] Verdict: Mostly false

## Say this

Every million spent on clean energy supports about three times the jobs it does in fossil fuels. The real issue is whether they are in the same places, and that needs actual transition funding.

## Why it sounds right

There is something real here, and it is not the total. Jobs lost and jobs created are rarely in the same towns, for the same people, at the same time. Dismissing that is how you lose the argument and deserve to.

## What is actually true

On the totals, clean energy is more labour intensive per pound spent. One study found $1 million of spending supports 7.49 full time jobs in renewables and 7.72 in energy efficiency, against 2.65 in fossil fuels, so each $1 million shifted from brown to green is a net gain of around five jobs. In the US, over $100 billion of announced EV investment is linked to more than 100,000 jobs, and Boston Consulting Group research finds EV assembly needs as much labour as combustion assembly. The specific claim that 40% of US auto jobs would vanish under EV rules was examined by FactCheck.org and found to have no supporting evidence.

## If they push back

**"Tell that to a refinery town"**

Completely fair, and that is the argument we should be having. Retraining and regional investment, not pretending the disruption is not real.

## Also heard as

- "40% of US auto jobs will disappear"
- "you're putting whole towns out of work"
- "green jobs don't pay"
