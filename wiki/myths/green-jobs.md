# Myth: The transition will destroy jobs

> [!WARNING] Verdict: Mostly false

## Say this

Every million spent on clean energy supports about three times as many jobs as it does in fossil fuels. The real question is whether those jobs land in the same places, and that takes proper transition funding.

## Why it sounds right

There is something real here, though it isn't about the total number of jobs. The jobs lost and the jobs created are rarely in the same towns, for the same people, at the same time. Brushing that aside is how you lose the argument, and it would be fair to lose it.

## What is actually true

Overall, clean energy creates more jobs for every pound spent. One study found $1 million of spending supports **7.49 full time jobs** in renewables and 7.72 in energy efficiency, against 2.65 in fossil fuels. So each $1 million moved from fossil fuels to clean energy adds around five jobs. In the US, over $100 billion of announced EV investment is linked to more than 100,000 jobs, and Boston Consulting Group research finds that building an EV takes as much labour as building a petrol or diesel car. FactCheck.org looked into the claim that 40% of US car industry jobs would vanish under EV rules and found no evidence to support it.

## If they push back

**"Tell that to a refinery town"**

That's completely fair, and it's the argument we should be having. The answer is retraining and investment in the regions affected, not pretending the disruption isn't real.

## Also heard as

- "40% of US auto jobs will disappear"
- "you're putting whole towns out of work"
- "green jobs don't pay"
