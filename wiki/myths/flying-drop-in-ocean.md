# Myth: My one flight makes no difference

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

Flying is the one choice where a single decision really does move your own number, because most people on earth never fly at all.

## Why it sounds right

Aviation is a small slice of global emissions, so at the global scale the number really is small. The distribution is what makes the argument fall apart.

## What is actually true

Flying is the fastest way for one person to spend their entire annual carbon budget, and it is extremely unequally spread: a small minority of frequent flyers accounts for the large majority of aviation emissions. New Scientist reporting found that high income, environmentally minded people tend to have larger footprints than their peers, mostly because they fly more. The industry's own plan is not on track either. In June 2026 IATA's chief Willie Walsh admitted the 2050 net zero pledge will probably not be achieved, with sustainable aviation fuel sitting at 0.8% of its target.

## If they push back

**"Sustainable fuel will fix it"**

The head of the airline industry said in 2026 that their 2050 target probably will not be met. Their green fuel is at under 1% of what was planned.

## Also heard as

- "the plane was going anyway"
- "aviation is only 2% of emissions"
- "they'll have clean fuel soon"
