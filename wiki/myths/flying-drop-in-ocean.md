# Myth: My one flight makes no difference

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

Flying is the one choice where a single decision really does change your own carbon footprint, because most people on earth never fly at all.

## Why it sounds right

Aviation is a small slice of global emissions, so at the global scale the number really is small. The problem with the argument is how unevenly that slice is shared out.

## What is actually true

Flying is the quickest way for one person to use up their whole yearly carbon budget, and it's very unequally spread: a small minority of frequent flyers accounts for most aviation emissions. New Scientist reporting found that people on high incomes who care about the environment tend to have larger footprints than their peers, mostly because they fly more. The industry's own plan is off track too. In June 2026 Willie Walsh, head of the airline body IATA, admitted the 2050 net zero pledge will probably not be met, with sustainable aviation fuel at just **0.8%** of its target.

## If they push back

**"Sustainable fuel will fix it"**

The head of the airline industry said in 2026 that its 2050 target probably won't be met. Its green fuel is at under 1% of what was planned.

## Also heard as

- "the plane was going anyway"
- "aviation is only 2% of emissions"
- "they'll have clean fuel soon"
