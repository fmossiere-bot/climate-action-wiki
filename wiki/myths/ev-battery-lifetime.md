# Myth: The battery dies after a few years and costs a fortune to replace

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

In a study of 15,000 EVs, only 1.5% had needed a new battery. Most cars past 100,000 miles still had 90% of their range.

## Why it sounds right

Almost everyone has had a phone whose battery died within two years, so it's a reasonable thing to assume. EV battery packs are managed and cooled very differently, though.

## What is actually true

An analysis of 15,000 EVs by Recurrent Motors found that only **1.5% had needed a battery replacement**. Of the cars past 100,000 miles, 90% still held at least 90% of their original range. Most manufacturers guarantee the battery pack for at least eight years. Nissan UK reported that almost all of the batteries it has ever made are still in cars, after twelve years of selling them.

## If they push back

**"And then it goes to landfill"**

It doesn't. Packs with half their capacity left are being reused as grid storage, and recyclers recover over 90% of the lithium.

## Also heard as

- "battery lifetime is horribly uncertain"
- "you'll need a new battery at 8 years"
- "the replacement costs more than the car is worth"

## Go deeper

Most manufacturers offer battery warranties of at least eight years, and EVs do not lose value any faster than conventional cars.

Even so, carmakers accept that buyers are still unsure about battery life. Chinese-owned UK brand MG says on its website: “Electric car battery life is one of the main factors that makes drivers reluctant to switch to an electric vehicle.”

UK motoring website Autocar notes that many “rumours and anecdotes” circulate about EV batteries failing “after a relatively short space of time”. It suggests people's experience with mobile phone batteries is one reason these ideas persist.

Autocar also says most batteries will last the lifetime of the car, and Tesla says its batteries are “designed to outlast the vehicle”. In Autocar's words:

> “The more electric cars that are out there and the longer they are run for, the more evidence is produced to show that the power pack will often last the lifetime of the car.”
