# Myth: It takes more energy to build a wind turbine than it ever produces

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

A wind turbine pays back the carbon it took to build in about seven months, then runs for twenty odd years.

## Why it sounds right

Manufacturing solar panels and turbines does produce carbon, and most of a turbine's lifetime emissions really do happen before it ever turns. That much is true.

## What is actually true

The payback is quick. A solar panel offsets its manufacturing emissions in four to eight months and then runs for 25 to 30 years. A wind turbine takes about seven months and runs for 20 to 25 years, generating close to zero pollution once it is spinning. So a turbine spends well over 95% of its working life producing energy that is pure gain.

## If they push back

**"What about when the wind isn't blowing"**

Different question, and a fair one. That is about storage and grid design, not about whether the turbine is worth building.

## Also heard as

- "solar panels never pay back their manufacturing"
- "they only work because of subsidies"
- "the carbon cost of building them cancels it out"
