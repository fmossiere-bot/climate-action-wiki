# Myth: China emits more than us, so why should we bother

> [!WARNING] Verdict: True but incomplete

## Say this

China emits the most in total, but about half as much per person as the US. It also installs more solar and wind than the rest of the world put together, so it isn't the one standing still.

## Why it sounds right

China is the largest annual emitter, at roughly **30% of global CO2**, and it is still building coal plants. Those facts are correct, and brushing them aside costs you credibility straight away.

## What is actually true

Per person, Chinese emissions are well below American ones. Historically, the US and Europe put up most of the CO2 already in the atmosphere, and it stays there for centuries, so much of the warming we have now comes from us. China also installs more solar and wind than the rest of the world combined, and it dominates the manufacture of the panels, batteries and EVs everyone else is buying. The idea that China is doing nothing has been out of date for years.

## If they push back

**"They're still building coal plants"**

They are, and it is a real problem. They are also building more clean power than everyone else combined. Both are true at once.

**"But we're tiny, it makes no difference"**

All the countries under 2% each add up to about a third of global emissions. If being small means you're exempt, a third of the problem is exempt.

## Also heard as

- "we're only 1% of global emissions"
- "China opens a coal plant every week"
- "nothing we do matters while they keep building"

## Go deeper

#### China's pollution in numbers

- China makes as much cement in **2 years** as the US made in the whole 20th century.
- In the last 8 years, China has released more CO2 than the UK has since the Industrial Revolution began.
- In 2023, China built two-thirds of all new coal plants built in the world.


#### The other side: China also leads on clean energy

##### Solar and wind power
- China builds enough solar and wind power in **one year** to run the whole UK.
- In 2023, China installed more solar power than the US has ever had in its entire history.
- China now produces more solar and wind power than Russia, Canada or Brazil use in total.

##### Electric cars
- Nearly 40% of new cars sold in China in 2023 were electric.
- By 2024, this was close to 50%.
- China is far ahead of the US and Europe, where electric car sales are still much lower.
- Some electric cars in China cost as little as $10,000.

##### Controlling the supply chain
- China makes about 80% of the world's solar panels.
- China makes about 80% of the world's lithium-ion batteries (used in EVs and storage).
- China makes about 40% of the world's wind turbines.

#### What about all those coal plants?

- China is still building coal plants, but that doesn't mean it will burn more coal.
- Power plants don't run all the time. They get switched on when needed.
- In the early 2000s, China's coal plants ran about 70% of the time. Now it's down to about 50%, and falling.
- Coal is becoming a backup, used to fill gaps when solar and wind aren't producing (much as the UK or US use gas).
- Climate is one reason for China's clean energy push. **Energy independence** is another: relying on other countries for fuel is risky, so China would rather build and source its own clean energy at home.

#### Where this leaves us

China's emissions don't have to keep rising. If its clean energy keeps growing fast enough to meet rising demand for power, coal use will fall. Because China is so big, when its emissions peak and start to drop, the world's emissions will follow.
