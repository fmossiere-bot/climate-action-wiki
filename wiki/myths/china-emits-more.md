# Myth: China emits more than us, so why should we bother

> [!WARNING] Verdict: True but incomplete

## Say this

China emits the most in total and about half as much per person as the US. It also installs more solar and wind than the rest of the world put together, so they are not the ones standing still.

## Why it sounds right

China is the largest annual emitter, at roughly 30% of global CO2, and it is still building coal plants. Those facts are correct and pretending otherwise costs you credibility instantly.

## What is actually true

Three things are missing. Per person, Chinese emissions are well below American ones. Historically, the US and Europe put up the majority of the CO2 already in the atmosphere, and it stays there for centuries, so the warming we have now is largely ours. And on action, China installs more solar and wind than the rest of the world combined and dominates manufacturing of the panels, batteries and EVs everyone else is buying. The premise that they are doing nothing has not been true for years.

## If they push back

**"They're still building coal plants"**

They are, and it is a real problem. They are also building more clean power than everyone else combined. Both are true at once.

**"But we're tiny, it makes no difference"**

Every country under 2% adds up to about a third of global emissions. If small means exempt, a third of the problem is exempt.

## Also heard as

- "we're only 1% of global emissions"
- "China opens a coal plant every week"
- "nothing we do matters while they keep building"
