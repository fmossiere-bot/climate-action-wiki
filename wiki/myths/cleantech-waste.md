# Myth: Wind turbine blades and solar panels just end up in landfill

> [!WARNING] Verdict: Mostly false

## Say this

Solar waste by 2050 is about 160 million tonnes. Plastic waste will be over 12 billion. It is a real problem, just not the one worth panicking about.

## Why it sounds right

There are real photographs of buried blades, and a wave of equipment installed over the last two decades is genuinely reaching end of life now. The problem is not imaginary.

## What is actually true

It is a real problem of a modest size. Cumulative solar panel waste could reach 160 million tonnes by 2050. By the same date total e-waste is projected at around 1.8 billion tonnes and plastic waste at over 12 billion tonnes, so solar is a rounding error next to the waste streams nobody campaigns about. Recycling is also scaling fast. China's GEM recovers over 90% of the lithium from used batteries and processed around 17,000 tonnes of EV batteries in 2022. Redwood Materials found many packs it takes in still hold more than half their capacity, and is repurposing them into solar powered microgrids rather than shredding them.

## If they push back

**"Recycling never actually happens"**

For plastic, largely fair. For batteries it is a genuine industry already, because the materials inside are valuable enough to chase.

## Also heard as

- "they're burying blades in the desert"
- "nobody can recycle solar panels"
- "the waste from clean tech will be the next crisis"
