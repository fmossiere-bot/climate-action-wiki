# Myth: Wind turbine blades and solar panels just end up in landfill

> [!WARNING] Verdict: Mostly false

## Say this

By 2050 the world will have about 160 million tonnes of old solar panels to deal with, against more than 12 billion tonnes of plastic waste. It's a real problem, but a small one, and recycling is already catching up.

## Why it sounds right

There are real photographs of buried turbine blades. A wave of equipment installed over the last two decades is now reaching the end of its life. The problem is not imaginary.

## What is actually true

It is a real problem of a modest size. Solar panel waste could add up to **160 million tonnes by 2050**. By the same date, total e-waste is projected at around 1.8 billion tonnes and plastic waste at over 12 billion tonnes, so solar is a rounding error next to waste streams nobody campaigns about.

Recycling is also growing fast, especially for batteries. China's GEM recovers over 90% of the lithium from used batteries and processed around 17,000 tonnes of EV batteries in 2022. Redwood Materials found that many packs it takes in still hold more than half their capacity, so it is turning them into solar-powered microgrids instead of shredding them.

## If they push back

**"Recycling never actually happens"**

For plastic, that's largely fair. For batteries it is already a real industry, because the materials inside are valuable enough to be worth recovering.

## Also heard as

## Go deeper

FACT: Clean energy technologies produce far less carbon pollution than fossil fuels
Argument is that producing those tech produce Carbon, which it does, but it is getting better and better, same as for Cars and Batteries. 
- Solar Panel for instance, only take 4-8 months to make up for their manufacturing emissions (and the average solar panel has a lifetime of around 25-30 years). 
- Wind turbines, similarly, take only about 7 months to produce enough clean electricity to make up for the carbon pollution generated during manufacture (and they have a typical lifespan of 20-25 years). [(ScienceDirect)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0960148111002254)Most of the carbon pollution generated during a wind turbine’s life occurs during manufacturing. Once it’s up and spinning, the turbine generates close to zero pollution
- Electric vehicles, over their lifecycle, from manufacturing to disposal, produce about half the carbon emissions of the average internal combustion engine car, with the potential for a further 25 per cent reduction with low-carbon electricity. [(IEA)](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-role-of-critical-minerals-in-clean-energy-transitions/executive-summary)<!-- Yours to expand.
