# Myth: Hydrogen or synthetic fuel is the real answer, not batteries

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

Hydrogen and e-fuels matter for planes, ships and steel. For cars they need three to five times more electricity to go the same distance, which is why there are 26 million EVs and 72,000 hydrogen cars.

## Why it sounds right

Both technologies are real and both matter, just not for cars. Hydrogen has a serious role in steel, shipping and long haul freight, and e-fuels will likely be needed for aviation.

## What is actually true

For passenger cars the energy maths is unforgiving. UK government lifecycle analysis finds hydrogen cars use about three times more energy than EVs with 60 to 70% higher emissions, even assuming low carbon hydrogen. The market has already answered: at the end of 2022 there were 72,000 hydrogen vehicles worldwide against 26 million EVs, a ratio of about 360 to 1. E-fuels cost up to three times more than conventional fuel, need roughly five times more electricity than running an EV, and have lifecycle emissions 53% higher. The IPCC expects the limited supply to go to aviation and shipping.

## If they push back

**"The technology will improve"**

Some, but the losses are thermodynamic, not engineering. You cannot make converting electricity to fuel and back beat using the electricity directly.

## Also heard as

- "hydrogen cars are more sustainable"
- "e-fuels will save the combustion engine"
- "we should wait for the better technology"
