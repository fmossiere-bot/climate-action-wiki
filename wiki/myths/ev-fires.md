# Myth: Electric cars catch fire and explode

> [!WARNING] Verdict: False

## Say this

EV fires make the news precisely because they are rare. Petrol and diesel cars catch fire five to six times more often per vehicle.

## Why it sounds right

Battery fires behave differently and are harder for firefighters to extinguish, so the concern is not invented. They are also very newsworthy, which is exactly why they feel common.

## What is actually true

Norwegian data for 2018 to 2022 records about 30 fires per 100,000 conventional cars a year against about 5 per 100,000 EVs, so petrol and diesel cars catch fire five to six times more often. The Swedish Civil Contingencies Agency puts the gap at twenty times. The EU funded LASH FIRE programme concluded EVs are not more hazardous than conventional cars. The Fremantle Highway cargo ship fire in July 2023 was widely blamed on an EV, and the investigation found the cause was unrelated. The International Union of Marine Insurers stated in August 2023 that no fire on a car carrier had ever been proven to be caused by a factory new EV.

## If they push back

**"But when they do burn you can't stop it"**

True, they are harder to put out. That is a firefighting equipment question, not a reason to think they burn more often.

## Also heard as

- "they burn down car parks"
- "that ship fire was caused by an EV"
- "you can't put the fire out"
