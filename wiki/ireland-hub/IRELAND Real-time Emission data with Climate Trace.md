# Ireland Real-Time Emission Data with Climate Trace

## What is it

[Climate TRACE](../concepts/key-ideas/The%20Al-Gore%20CLIMATE%20TRACE%20is%20live.md) is a real-time, satellite-based global emissions monitoring tool, co-founded by Al Gore, that went live with monthly data updates in 2025. Looking at Ireland's data on this new tool confirms three things about the country's emissions profile.

## What Climate TRACE Shows for Ireland

1. **Agriculture remains Ireland's largest source of emissions**, at over 40% of the total (this figure excludes land use and forestry).
2. **Ireland's emissions may have been underestimated** by official inventories: Climate TRACE data shows around 78 million tonnes, compared with the roughly 60 million tonnes reported for 2021 under previous accounting.
3. **Transport is also a major contributor**, accounting for around 20% of emissions.

## Why it matters

Climate TRACE's approach differs fundamentally from traditional emissions accounting, which has historically relied on self-reported data from polluters themselves and inventories compiled years after the fact. Climate TRACE instead uses satellites, sensors and artificial intelligence to deliver verified emissions estimates with only a 60-day lag, monitoring over 660 million individual emission sources worldwide, including power plants, factories, farms and shipping vessels. If Ireland's true emissions are indeed higher than official figures suggest, that has direct implications for how much further and how much faster the country needs to act to meet its climate targets.
