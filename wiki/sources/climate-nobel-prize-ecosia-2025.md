# Ecosia pushes for a Climate Nobel Prize

**Source:** Good Good Good, Kamrin Baker, 11 December 2025. https://www.goodgoodgood.co/articles/nobel-prize-in-climate-and-planetary-health-ecosia

## What it is

A short article on a campaign by Ecosia, the search engine that plants trees, to create a new Nobel Prize for Climate and Planetary Health. Ecosia's CEO, Christian Kroll, has signed a **€1 million** commitment before a notary in Berlin to fund the prize's first endowment. He has also written to the Nobel Foundation to explore making it official.

The proposal would add a 7th Nobel category. It suggests three areas the prize could reward: Pragmatic Governance, Scaling Prosperity and Markets, and Building Common Ground. Ecosia says it would have no say over who is nominated or who wins.

## Why it matters

Prizes shape what people see as important work. The campaign points to the economics Nobel, added in 1969, which reflected the priorities of that time. Giving climate leadership the same standing, it argues, would encourage more of it.

It is a symbolic push rather than a done deal. The Nobel Foundation has not agreed to anything, and the article reports a pledge and a request, not a decision.

## Key facts

- Ecosia pledged a €1m endowment, signed before a notary in Berlin, and says it is open to adding more support over time.
- The proposal is for a 7th Nobel category, Climate and Planetary Health.
- Three suggested recognition areas: Pragmatic Governance, Scaling Prosperity and Markets, and Building Common Ground.
- Ecosia would have no influence over nominations or winners.
- A symbolic blue chair was made and placed at the Nobel ceremony.
- Backers include the activist Luisa Neubauer and the energy economist Dr Claudia Kemfert.
- The campaign compares itself to the economics Nobel, introduced in 1969.
- Campaign website: climatenobelprize.org
