# AMOC monitoring under threat

**Source:** The Guardian, 14 June 2026. Written by Penny Holliday (National Oceanography Centre), Dr M Femke de Jong (NIOZ) and Dr Sjoerd Groeskamp (NIOZ). https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/jun/14/amoc-collapse-europe-climate

## What it is

Three senior oceanographers wrote this comment piece for the Guardian. Their worry is simple: the instruments that watch the **AMOC** (the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation, the great ocean current that carries warm water north towards Europe) are losing their funding just when we most need them.

If the AMOC weakens badly or collapses, Europe could see its climate change up to 10 times faster than it is changing today.

## Why it matters

We only have about 20 years of steady measurements of this current. They were pieced together from research projects that each country paid for separately, so there was never one secure budget behind them. Now about half of that money is at risk because of cuts in the United States.

The cost of keeping watch is small. All AMOC monitoring together costs about €25m a year, roughly 5 cents for each person in the EU. The authors ask the EU, the UK and international partners to fund it for the long term and to keep the data open to everyone.

They also make a point about how to read the science. Recent studies disagree on whether the AMOC has already weakened. That reflects real uncertainty in the measurements, not a split among scientists, and it is a reason to keep measuring rather than stop.

## Key facts

- If the AMOC collapses, Europe could experience climate change **10 times faster** than the current rate.
- Regular monitoring of the AMOC began only about 20 years ago, built from nationally funded research projects.
- Budget cuts by the Trump administration to NASA, NOAA and NSF affect about 50% of all AMOC monitoring funding.
- In June 2026 the US also announced it was scaling back the Ocean Observatories Initiative.
- The EU's OceanEye initiative has €50m committed, but it is not yet running.
- All AMOC monitoring costs about €25m a year, around 5 cents per EU citizen per year.
