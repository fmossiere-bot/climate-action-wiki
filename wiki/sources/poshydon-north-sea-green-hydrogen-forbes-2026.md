# PosHYdon produces first green hydrogen on a North Sea gas platform

**Source:** Forbes, Gaurav Sharma, 11 August 2026 — https://www.forbes.com/sites/gauravsharma/2026/08/11/dutch-project-produces-first-green-hydrogen-on-north-sea-gas-platform/

## What it is

The **PosHYdon** pilot project produced its first batch of green hydrogen in late July 2026 on the **Q13a-A platform**, an active natural gas platform operated by Eni Energy Netherlands roughly 13km off Scheveningen in the Dutch North Sea. It is described as the world's first project to combine offshore wind power, green hydrogen production and ongoing natural gas extraction on the same platform.

## Key facts cited

- **Q13a-A platform**: the first "fully electrified production platform" in the Dutch North Sea sector
- Process: offshore wind electricity converts seawater into demineralised water, then into hydrogen via electrolysis, while gas extraction continues simultaneously
- Project began in 2022; consortium includes Eni, DEME Group, EBN, Eneco, Emerson, Gasunie, Hatenboerwater, Investa, Iv, Nel, Nexstep, NGT, Nogat, TAQA and TNO, with support from the Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs and Climate Policy
- Framed by partners as a "watershed moment" for reusing existing offshore oil-and-gas infrastructure for renewable energy production
- Emerson's automation systems manage the integration of desalination, electrolysis and gas-blending processes
- Purpose of the pilot: gather real operational data on **cost, maintenance and performance**, to be shared later in 2026, to help scale up offshore hydrogen production based on offshore wind

## Why it matters

Most green hydrogen discussion in the wiki's [FACTS about Clean & GREEN Hydrogen](../solutions/renewables/FACTS%20about%20Clean%20&%20GREEN%20Hydrogen.md) page is about hydrogen's storage and industrial-decarbonisation potential in the abstract. PosHYdon is a concrete, small-scale but real-world case of **offshore infrastructure reuse**: instead of building new platforms for hydrogen, an active gas platform is retrofitted to also make hydrogen from wind power, offering a template for lower-cost offshore hydrogen scale-up as the North Sea's gas platforms age.
