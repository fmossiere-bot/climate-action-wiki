# PosHYdon produces first green hydrogen on a North Sea gas platform

**Source:** Forbes, Gaurav Sharma, 11 August 2026. https://www.forbes.com/sites/gauravsharma/2026/08/11/dutch-project-produces-first-green-hydrogen-on-north-sea-gas-platform/

## What it is

**PosHYdon** is a Dutch pilot project that made its first batch of green hydrogen in late July 2026. It runs on the Q13a-A platform, a working natural gas platform operated by Eni Energy Netherlands about 13km off Scheveningen in the Dutch North Sea.

It is described as the first project in the world to combine offshore wind power, green hydrogen production and ongoing gas extraction on one platform. Green hydrogen is hydrogen made by splitting water with renewable electricity, so producing it releases no CO2.

## Why it matters

Much of the talk about green hydrogen stays general: it could store energy and help clean up heavy industry. PosHYdon is a small but real test. Instead of building new platforms, it adds hydrogen production to one that already exists and keeps running on gas.

Many North Sea gas platforms are ageing. If they can be reused this way, offshore hydrogen could grow at lower cost, and some of the old fossil fuel infrastructure gets a second life.

## Key facts

- Q13a-A is the first "fully electrified production platform" in the Dutch part of the North Sea.
- The process uses electricity from offshore wind to turn seawater into demineralised water, then split it into hydrogen by electrolysis. Gas extraction carries on at the same time.
- The project started in 2022. The consortium includes Eni, DEME Group, EBN, Eneco, Emerson, Gasunie, Hatenboerwater, Investa, Iv, Nel, Nexstep, NGT, Nogat, TAQA and TNO, with support from the Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs and Climate Policy.
- The partners call it a "watershed moment" for reusing offshore oil and gas infrastructure to produce renewable energy.
- Emerson's automation systems tie together desalination, electrolysis and gas blending.
- The aim is to gather real operating data on **cost, maintenance and performance**, to be shared later in 2026, to help scale up offshore hydrogen made from offshore wind.
