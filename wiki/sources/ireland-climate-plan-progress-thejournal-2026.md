# Ireland has completed just 10 of 74 climate commitments

**Source:** TheJournal.ie, Jane Matthews, 29 July 2026 — https://www.thejournal.ie/climate-targets-progress-report-7116989-Jul2026/

## What it is

A progress report published by Ireland's Department of Climate and the Environment finds the government has completed only **10 of the 74 measures** committed to across the 2023, 2024 and 2025 Climate Action Plans. The 2026 plan itself was still unpublished at the time of writing, delayed to Q3 2026, which opposition politicians called "inexcusable."

## Key facts cited

- **10 of 74** measures completed over three years (2023-2025 plans combined)
- Completed items include: publication of the second Circular Economy Strategy, 200km of cycling infrastructure delivered, a new national demand management strategy for transport
- Department attributes delays in the other **64 measures** to capacity constraints, consultation requirements, funding/procurement issues, and alignment with other national or EU policy
- Warning: delays threaten Ireland's ability to meet binding emissions targets, risk further climate change, and increase the future cost of climate-related spending
- **Agriculture** was Ireland's highest-emitting sector in 2025, responsible for **36% of all national emissions**; methane made up **70%** of agricultural emissions, mostly from cattle, sheep and manure management
- Neither of the two agriculture-specific 2023-2025 commitments has been delivered: a framework to reduce feed costs in organic livestock, and a draft carbon farming framework (now over two years late, due later in 2026)
- Other undelivered measures: shared mobility hubs in four cities/towns; ~225 EV charging points at sports clubs; a roadmap to phase out fossil fuel heating in all buildings; an OPW retrofit programme for traditional/historic buildings
- Reactions: Taoiseach Micheál Martin called it "a clear reminder that we must move faster"; Climate Minister Darragh O'Brien said there is "no room for delay"; Labour's Ciarán Ahern said it shows "just how far away from achieving our climate action targets this government is"
