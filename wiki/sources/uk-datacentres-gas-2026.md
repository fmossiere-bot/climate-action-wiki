# More than 100 UK datacentres plan to burn gas to generate electricity

**Source:** The Guardian, Aisha Down, 18 May 2026. https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/may/18/uk-datacentres-plan-to-burn-gas-to-generate-electricity

## What it is

The Guardian reports that more than 100 planned UK data centres have asked to be connected to the gas network. They cannot get a connection to the electricity grid quickly enough, so they want to make their own power by burning gas. In some cases this would be their main, permanent power supply rather than a backup.

## Why it matters

The UK has set itself a goal called **Clean Power 2030**: by 2030, gas burned without capturing its emissions should make up less than 5% of the electricity system. Data centres running permanently on gas pull in the opposite direction, and could knock that target off course.

It also shows a wider problem. Data centres, many of them built for AI, are arriving faster than grids can connect them. When the clean option is slow, developers reach for fossil fuels, and those plants can lock in emissions for years.

## Key facts

- 100 GW of data centre projects are waiting to connect to the UK's National Grid, according to the energy regulator Ofgem.
- Data centre operators made more than 100 requests for gas connections in two years, according to Future Energy Networks.
- Together these requests come to more than **15 TWh of gas energy a year**, enough to power London for about 4.5 months.
- Some developers want more than 100 MW of gas power on a permanent basis.
- The UK's Clean Power 2030 target aims for less than 5% unabated gas (gas burned without capturing its emissions) in the electricity system.
- In the US, 11 data centres built for Meta, OpenAI, Microsoft and xAI will reportedly emit more CO₂ than Morocco.
