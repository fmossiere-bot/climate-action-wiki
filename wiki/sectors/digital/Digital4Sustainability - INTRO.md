# Digital4Sustainability: Introduction

## What is it

The **Digital4Sustainability Learning Path** is a course exploring the transformational role digital solutions and innovations can play in advancing environmental and social sustainability, in particular how digital technologies can power the green transition, and how the digital transition itself can be made green. It was developed through a partnership between the UN Environment Programme (UNEP) and the UN System Staff College (UNSSC), in collaboration with GIZ, the EU, the Coalition for Digital Environmental Sustainability (CODES) and the Office of the UN Technology Envoy. Its content is inspired by the CODES Action Plan for a Sustainable Planet in the Digital Age.

The course is built around four modules, released as a series:

- **Module 1: Digital Transformation for Sustainable Development** introduces digital transformation, walking through relevant terminology and emerging trends, and explores challenges and entry points for effective action with real-world examples.
- **Module 2: Digital Sustainability for Climate Action** explores the role of digital transformation in tackling human-induced climate change, covering key opportunities and threats for both climate mitigation and adaptation.
- **Module 3: Digital Sustainability for Biodiversity Action** looks at the opportunities and challenges of using digital innovation for biodiversity action, covering conservation, restoration and sustainable use.
- **Module 4: Digital Sustainability for Pollution Action** covers the role of digital technologies in combatting pollution, and how emerging technologies can accelerate systemic change to prevent it.

## Why it matters

People now spend more than a third of their lives in front of screens. That scale of dependence means digitalisation is not a neutral background trend, it is already reshaping how much energy and material the world consumes, and how effectively (or not) societies respond to the climate, nature and pollution crises. This course frames digital transformation as a double-edged tool: it can accelerate solutions to the Triple Planetary Crisis of climate change, nature loss and pollution, or, left unchecked, it can accelerate the drivers of that same crisis.

## Objectives

Upon completion of the learning path, participants should understand:

- Key concepts, including digitalisation, digital transformation, and digital sustainability
- The role of digital transformation in countering the Triple Planetary Crisis of climate change, nature loss and pollution
- How to leverage the potential of digital transformation in advancing sustainability using a systems approach
- The negative impacts of digital transformation, and how to mitigate them to ensure sustainability
