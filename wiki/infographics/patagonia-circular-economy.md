# Patagonia's circular economy: how it keeps gear in use for longer

![Circular diagram around a Patagonia fleece jacket showing nine stages of a product's life, with Patagonia's figures for each stage.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fmossiere-bot/climate-action-wiki/refs/heads/main/wiki/infographics/images/patagonia-circular-economy.webp)

*Infographic by [Antonio Vizcaya Abdo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/antoniovizcaya/), shared on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7520665625732653056/). Data: Patagonia, Work in Progress Report (November 2025). Images: Patagonia.*

## What it shows
The outdoor clothing brand Patagonia tries to keep its products in use for as long as possible, at nine stages of a product's life: buying less, designing to last, better materials, cutting factory and packaging waste, repair, resale, remaking and recycling. All the figures are Patagonia's own, from its 2025 report. The most striking one is an admission: for 85 to 90% of what it sells, there is still no good end-of-life solution.

## Key figures
- 212,000 used products resold through Worn Wear in FY25 (Patagonia, Work in Progress Report 2025)
- 174,799 items repaired in FY25 (Patagonia, Work in Progress Report 2025)
- 85 to 90% of the products Patagonia puts into the world lack an end-of-life solution, mainly because mixed materials are hard to recycle (Patagonia, Work in Progress Report 2025)
- 150+ styles use NetPlus, a material made from recycled fishing nets (Patagonia, Work in Progress Report 2025)
- 40 million+ fewer hangtags used (Patagonia, Work in Progress Report 2025)
- A redesigned duffel bag's quality score rose from 7.2 to 9.3 after changes for easier repair (Patagonia, Work in Progress Report 2025)
- Patagonia has repaired gear since 1976 and ran its first garment recycling programme in 2005 (Patagonia, Work in Progress Report 2025)
